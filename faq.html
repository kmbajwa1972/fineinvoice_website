Frequently Asked Questions

Here are answers to common questions about FineInvoice and its invoice tools.

Is FineInvoice free?

New accounts receive 3 free PDF downloads to try the service. FineInvoice also offers paid options for additional PDF access. Current pricing is shown on the FineInvoice homepage.

Can I create PDF invoices?

Yes. FineInvoice lets you create professional invoices and download PDF files. You can review the invoice before downloading it.

Do I need a credit card to start?

No. You can create an account and use the free trial without a credit card.

Who can use FineInvoice?

FineInvoice is designed for freelancers, agencies, consultants, contractors, and small businesses.

Which currencies are supported?

The current invoice builder supports PKR, USD, EUR, AED, and GBP.

Can I save customer information?

Yes. Customer management is available so you can reuse customer details when preparing future invoices.

Can I create receipts?

Yes. FineInvoice includes a receipt maker for creating professional payment receipts.

Can I customize invoices?

FineInvoice provides invoice templates and color themes. Paid access includes additional templates and customization options shown on the pricing section.

Does FineInvoice provide accounting or tax advice?

No. FineInvoice is an invoicing tool. Tax rules and invoice requirements vary by location and business type, so users should obtain professional advice when necessary.

How do I get help?

Email support@fineinvoice.com or visit the Contact page.
