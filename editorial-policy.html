FineInvoice Editorial Policy

Publisher and operator: Khalid Mehmood · Last updated: November 10, 2026

This policy explains how FineInvoice prepares and maintains the educational material published on fineinvoice.com. It applies to our invoice guides, FAQs, examples, checklists and downloadable resources.

Who publishes the content

FineInvoice is owned and operated by Khalid Mehmood, who is responsible for the public educational content, product information and editorial corrections published on this website. Our guides are product-publisher resources written to help freelancers and small businesses understand practical invoicing workflows. They are not represented as independent legal, tax, accounting or financial advice, and we do not claim that every article has been individually reviewed by an external professional.

How topics are selected

We focus on questions that arise during everyday billing work: what to include on an invoice, how to number documents, how to calculate totals, how to record client details, how to follow up on late payment, and when to issue a receipt. We prioritize material that helps a reader complete a task or make a clearer decision.

Examples, calculations and templates

Examples may use fictional names, businesses, addresses and amounts to demonstrate a process without disclosing a real customer's information. Calculations are included to make the steps checkable. Spreadsheet and document downloads are intended as editable starting points; users should replace sample data and verify formulas and document details before using them in business.

Sources and jurisdiction-specific information

Where a guide discusses legal, tax, VAT, GST, record-retention or regulatory matters, the requirements can depend on the reader's location and circumstances. We aim to distinguish general explanations from jurisdiction-specific requirements and encourage readers to confirm current rules with the relevant government authority or a qualified adviser. A general example should not be treated as a statement of the law in every country.

Updates and maintenance

We update a page when a product workflow, download, calculation example, link or other material detail changes, or when a reader report identifies something that needs correction. The displayed update date should reflect a substantive review or change, not merely a formatting edit. We also check internal links and downloads as part of site maintenance.

Corrections and reader feedback

If you find a calculation error, broken download, outdated instruction or unclear statement, email support@fineinvoice.com. Include the page URL and a short description of the issue. Do not include passwords, payment-card details or confidential customer records. We review reports and make corrections where appropriate.

Product and commercial transparency

FineInvoice publishes the guides and also offers invoicing software. Some pages link to FineInvoice tools where those tools are relevant to the task being explained. Product links are intended to identify an available workflow, not to replace the educational explanation. Public pages may contain advertising; advertising does not determine the editorial conclusions or calculation examples in a guide.

Limits of our content

Our guides provide general educational information. They cannot account for every contract, tax status, industry rule or country-specific requirement. Users remain responsible for checking the accuracy and suitability of an invoice or business decision for their circumstances.

Contact

For editorial corrections or questions about the website, contact support@fineinvoice.com or visit our Contact page. Learn more about the service on the About FineInvoice page.
