Cookie Policy

Last updated: September 2026

FineInvoice uses cookies and similar technologies to support website functionality, remember preferences, help secure the service, and understand how public pages are used.

What are cookies?

Cookies are small text files or similar browser storage mechanisms that websites can use to remember information between visits. Some are necessary for a website to work correctly, while others support optional functions such as analytics.

How FineInvoice may use them

  • To keep website features working correctly.
  • To remember user preferences or temporary settings.
  • To support authentication and account security where required.
  • To understand public-page usage and improve performance.
  • To help protect the website from abuse or suspicious activity.

Analytics

Analytics technologies may collect information about visits, pages viewed, browser or device characteristics, and general usage patterns. This information helps us understand which public content is useful and where the website can be improved.

Advertising

FineInvoice may use Google AdSense or related Google advertising technologies after the website is approved and ads are enabled. Advertising services may use cookies or similar technologies according to their own policies and the choices available to users.

Managing cookies

You can control or delete cookies through your browser settings. Blocking essential cookies may affect account access or other website functions.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when our website, services, or legal requirements change. The latest version will be published on this page.

Contact

Questions about cookies can be sent to support@fineinvoice.com.
