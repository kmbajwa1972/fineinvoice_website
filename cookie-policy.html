Cookie Policy

FineInvoice uses cookies and similar technologies to improve website functionality, security, analytics, and user experience.

What are cookies?

Cookies are small text files stored on your device when you visit a website. They help websites remember preferences and provide better services.

How we use cookies

  • To keep the website working correctly.
  • To remember user preferences.
  • To understand website usage and improve performance.

Managing cookies

You can control or delete cookies through your browser settings. Disabling cookies may affect some website features.
