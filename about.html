About FineInvoice

FineInvoice is an invoicing tool built for freelancers, agencies, consultants, and small businesses that want a straightforward way to create professional invoices and payment documents.

What FineInvoice does

FineInvoice brings common invoicing tasks into one simple workflow. Users can create customer records, prepare invoices, review a live invoice layout, generate PDF documents, and keep invoice information available for future work. The service also supports multiple currencies and provides invoice templates for different types of independent work.

Why we built it

Many small businesses do not need complicated accounting software just to send a professional invoice. FineInvoice focuses on the everyday billing task: describe the work, calculate the amount due, present it clearly, and give the customer useful payment information.

Who it is for

FineInvoice is designed for people who bill customers directly, including freelancers, designers, writers, developers, consultants, agencies, contractors, and small business owners. The tool can also be useful for occasional invoicing when a full accounting platform would be more than you need.

Our approach

We aim to keep the interface clear and practical. Features are introduced around common billing workflows rather than technical accounting terminology. Pricing is presented openly on the website, with a free option for trying the service and paid options for additional PDF access.

Important note

FineInvoice is an invoicing tool, not a replacement for professional tax, legal, or accounting advice. Local requirements for invoices, taxes, record retention, and business registration vary. Users should make sure their invoices meet the rules that apply to their own business and location.
